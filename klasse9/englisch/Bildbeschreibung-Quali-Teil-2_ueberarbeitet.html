Useful words

to keep fit – fit bleiben
exhausting – anstrengend

Mini talk

  • Describe one sports picture.
  • Explain if it is a team sport or an individual sport.
  • Say why sport can be healthy.
  • Give your opinion: Is winning important?
Possible questions
What kind of sport can you see?
What are the people doing?
Do you prefer team sports or individual sports?
Why is sport good for teenagers?
What can be difficult about this sport?
How important is fair play?
Example start:
In this picture I can see people doing sport. They seem active and focused. I think this activity is good for teenagers because they can keep fit, meet friends and learn teamwork.

Mini talk

  • Choose one Australia picture.
  • Describe the place and the atmosphere.
  • Say what tourists could do there.
  • Explain if you would like to visit Australia.
Possible questions
What is Australia famous for?
What animals live in Australia?
Would you prefer visiting a city or nature?
What could tourists do at the beach?
What might be difficult about travelling to Australia?
Example start:
In this picture I can see a beautiful place in Australia. The landscape looks sunny and impressive. Australia is famous for its wildlife, beaches and big cities like Sydney. I would like to visit it because it seems very different from Germany.
